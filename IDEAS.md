## Next up
- **Shop phase B — seller products and orders.** Vendors add products from their dashboard
  ("Products" tab, sent for review like listings). Couples place an order → seller confirms →
  buyer pays the seller directly (UPI / payment link) → shipped with tracking → delivered.
  Zenfest's commission (about 15–25%) is worked out per delivered order and billed to the
  seller monthly; unpaid dues pause the seller's products. "My orders" in the couple account.
- **Grievance officer name** on `/shop/policies` (E-commerce Rules want a named person).

- **Verified reviews** — only couples who booked a vendor (or bought a product) through Zenfest
  can review.
